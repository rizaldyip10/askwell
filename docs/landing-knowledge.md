# Askwell — Product Guide

## What Askwell is

Askwell is an AI support chat widget for any website. You add your product information as text or files. You paste one script tag into your site. A chat button then appears on your site, and it answers visitor questions using only the information you gave it.

The bot is instructed to answer only from your information, and to say it doesn't know when the answer isn't there.

## Who it's for

- Small businesses and startups that want to answer common customer questions around the clock.
- Product, SaaS and e-commerce teams that want fewer repeat support questions.
- Anyone with a website and a set of product docs, FAQs or policies.

You don't need to write code. If you can paste a line of HTML into your site, you can install Askwell.

## How it works (3 steps)

1. **Add your knowledge.** Sign up, create a project, and add your product info. Paste text or upload a PDF, Markdown or TXT file.
2. **Install the widget.** Copy the script tag from the Install page and paste it into your website's HTML.
3. **Visitors get answers.** A chat button appears on your site. Visitors ask questions, and Askwell answers from your knowledge. You can read every conversation in your dashboard.

## Features

### Projects
- A project is one chatbot for one website, with its own knowledge, widget and conversations.
- Create, rename and delete projects from the dashboard.
- Free includes 1 project. Pro includes 5.

### Knowledge
- Add knowledge by pasting text with a title (up to 50,000 characters per text), or by uploading a file.
- Supported file types: PDF, Markdown (.md) and plain text (.txt).
- Maximum file size: 10 MB.
- Each file or pasted text counts as one knowledge source. Free includes 3 sources per project. Pro includes 25.
- Each source shows its status (processing, ready or failed). Delete a source at any time; its content is removed from the bot's knowledge.

### Chat widget
- A chat button sits in a corner of your site. Clicking it opens a chat panel.
- The widget is isolated from your page: it doesn't change your site's styles or layout.
- Answers come only from the knowledge in that project.
- Visitor messages can be up to 1,000 characters.
- Returning visitors on the same browser continue as the same visitor.
- The widget is protected against spam, bots and attempts to trick the AI.

### Conversations
- See every chat for each project, newest first, with a preview of the first message, the message count and the time.
- Open a conversation to read the full thread, visitor and bot.
- Refresh the page to see new conversations.

### Customize (Pro)
- Change the button color, button position (any corner), button label, open animation (fade, slide or scale), and the header text.
- Hide the "Powered by Askwell" badge.
- See a live preview as you edit. Changes show on your site the next time the page loads.
- On Free, the Customize page is visible but locked, with an upgrade option.

### Usage
- See how many messages each project has used this month, against its limit.
- The count resets every month.
- When a project reaches its monthly limit, the widget tells visitors it can't answer right now.

## Install steps

1. Sign up and create a project.
2. Add at least one knowledge source and wait until it shows "ready".
3. Open the **Install** page of your project.
4. Click **Copy** to copy your script tag. It looks like this:
   `<script src="https://.../widget.js" data-key="pk_..."></script>`
5. Paste it into your website's HTML, just before the closing `</body>` tag. It works on any site where you can add a custom script tag, for example plain HTML, WordPress, Webflow, Shopify, Squarespace, Wix, or a custom app.
6. Reload your site. The chat button appears in the corner.

The key in the tag is a public key. It's safe to put on your website.

## Upgrading, billing and cancelling

- Upgrade from the **Billing** page in your dashboard. Pay by card in the secure checkout.
- Pro features unlock as soon as the payment goes through.
- Manage or cancel your subscription from **Manage subscription** on the Billing page.
- If you cancel, you keep Pro until the end of the period you already paid for. Then your account returns to Free.

## What Askwell doesn't do

- **No DOCX or spreadsheet uploads.** Use PDF, Markdown or TXT, or paste the text.
- **No website scraping.** Askwell doesn't crawl your site. Add your content as text or files.
- **No buying-intent or lead scoring.**
- **No live conversation view.** Refresh the Conversations page to see new chats.
- **No human takeover.** You can't jump into a chat and reply as a person.
- **No choice of AI model.** Askwell uses one AI model for every project.
- **No teams.** Each account has one member. You can't invite teammates.

## FAQ

**What is Askwell?**
An AI chat widget for your website that answers visitor questions from the product information you give it.

**Does it work on my website?**
Yes, if you can add a line of HTML to your pages. That includes plain HTML sites, WordPress, Webflow, Shopify, Squarespace, Wix and custom web apps.

**Do I need to know how to code?**
No. You copy one script tag and paste it into your site.

**What files can I upload?**
PDF, Markdown (.md) and plain text (.txt), up to 10 MB each. You can also paste text directly, up to 50,000 characters.

**Can I upload Word documents or spreadsheets?**
Not yet. Save them as PDF or paste the text instead.

**Can Askwell read my website automatically?**
No. Askwell doesn't scrape websites. Add your content as text or files.

**What happens if the bot doesn't know the answer?**
It's instructed to say it doesn't know. It's told to answer only from your knowledge, not from general knowledge. Like any AI, it can occasionally get things wrong, so check your conversations now and then.

**How much does it cost?**
Free costs nothing and needs no credit card. It includes 1 project, 100 messages per month and 3 knowledge sources. Pro includes 5 projects, 2,000 messages per month per project, 25 knowledge sources per project and full customization. The current Pro price is on the pricing section and the Billing page.

**What happens when I hit my message limit?**
The widget tells visitors it can't answer right now. Your count resets at the start of the next month, or you can upgrade to Pro for 2,000 messages per month.

**Can I cancel anytime?**
Yes. Cancel from Manage subscription on the Billing page. You keep Pro until the end of your paid period, then move to Free.

**Can I change how the widget looks?**
On Pro, yes: button color, position, label, open animation, header text, and you can hide the "Powered by Askwell" badge. Free uses the default look.

**Is my data private?**
Your projects, knowledge and conversations are visible only to you in your dashboard. Each bot answers only from its own project's knowledge, never from another customer's. When you delete a knowledge source, its content is removed from the bot.

**Can I see what visitors asked?**
Yes. The Conversations page shows every chat in full, for each project.

**Can I invite my team?**
Not yet. Each account has one member.

**How do I sign up?**
Click "Start free" on the landing page. Sign up with email and password or with Google.
